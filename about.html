About Utrujj

To empower individuals to learn, love, and live Islam.

Utrujj, founded by Shaykh Haytham Tamim, is an Islamic educational institute that has been serving the UK Muslim community since 2001. Our work is founded on authentic scholarship, showing how traditional Islamic wisdom can help us deal with the realities of modern life.

Meet our founder Explore our classes

Who we are

Knowledge that changes lives.

We change lives by teaching Islam through online classes, courses and retreats, equipping individuals with authentic Islamic knowledge and spiritual tools to deepen their faith, refine their character, and ‘live Islam’ with confidence.

We believe that learning Islam is more than gaining knowledge — it is about becoming the best version of ourselves. Our mission is to inspire goodness, foster tolerance, and promote harmony within individuals, families, and communities.

Through the Quran and sunnah, we empower individuals to become catalysts for goodness, spreading kindness, integrity, and positive change in their communities.

Our founder

A lifelong seeker of knowledge.

Shaykh Haytham Tamim is a scholar who has dedicated his life to making sacred, authentic Islamic knowledge accessible and practical. By the grace of Allah, he has been disseminating traditional Islamic learning in the UK for over two decades.

With more than 30 years of expertise across major schools of thought and Islamic disciplines, his unbroken chain of scholarship traces directly back to the Prophet ﷺ.
