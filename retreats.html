Retreats

A journey of connection and renewal.

For over two decades, we have been organising spiritually enriching retreats — a unique opportunity to deepen faith, strengthen family bonds, and find inspiration in a tranquil, halal environment.

Retreat 2025

Re-imagining Prophetic vision.

We recently held our 25th Anniversary Retreat, marking a special milestone. With Allah’s grace, the weekend was inspiring, deeply motivational and very topical and relevant.

Attendees commented on the soothing atmosphere, as well as the buzz of meeting like-minded souls seeking to deepen their connection with Allah. There were moments of seriousness and depth, as well as lighthearted fun at the lively quiz night. There was something for all ages, as adults and children benefited from the thought-provoking talks and engaging workshops.

The central theme was well-researched and delivered with clarity, leaving participants feeling blessed with plenty to reflect on.

A rich history

Places we have gathered.

From Leeds to Wales, London to De Vere Woking, our gatherings have provided a sanctuary for spiritual reflection, growth and learning, guided by distinguished scholars.

2025

Re-Imagining Prophetic Vision

Burleigh Court Hotel, Loughborough

Reflecting on the prophetic model of building a just, ethical and successful civilisation.

2023

The Farewell Sermon

De Vere Woking, Surrey

Reflecting on the Prophet’s final sermon (peace be upon him) and its timeless legacy.

2019

Maqasid by Imam Nawawi

De Vere Woking, Surrey

Al-Maqāṣid by Imam al-Nawawi presents the fundamentals and pillars of Islam in a concise and clear manner.

2018

The 7 Dimensions of Ramadan

Gilwell Park, Chingford, London

A multi-faceted approach to preparing for Ramadan through reflection, worship, and knowledge.

2018

The Seventy-Seven Branches of Faith (Part 2)

Markfield Conference Centre, Leicestershire

Examined the characteristics of a strong believer and practical applications.

2017

The Seventy-Seven Branches of Faith (Part 1)

Markfield Conference Centre, Leicestershire

Unpacked the essential traits of faith and how to cultivate them in daily life.

2016

Journey of the Soul (Part 2)

Markfield Conference Centre, Leicestershire

Continuing the exploration of spiritual wisdom, focusing on the soul’s journey through life.

2015

Journey of the Soul (Part 1)

Markfield Conference Centre, Leicestershire

A reflection on Kitab Al-Hikam, guiding seekers through self-purification and closeness to Allah.

2012

The Prophet’s Counsel (peace be upon him)

Markfield Conference Centre, Leicestershire

An in-depth investigation of the wisdom-filled hadith, Forty Wasiyyah from Rasulullah (peace be upon him) to his Ummah, collected by Imam Abdullah Sirajuddin.

2012

In the Company of Muhammad (peace be upon him)

Buckland Hall, Brecon Beacons, Wales

Utrujj’s first five-day retreat immersed attendees in the life and teachings of the Prophet (peace be upon him) through the Shama’il by Tirmidhi, which was read in Arabic and English and attendees received Ijazahs.
