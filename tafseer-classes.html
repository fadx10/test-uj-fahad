The class

Why study tafseer?

The Quran is your divine guide to a successful life — but how much are you benefiting from it? Reciting it beautifully is one thing, but understanding its deeper meanings is another. Translations may help, yet without context, the coherence can be lost.

The Quran isn’t a simple narrative. It requires guidance. You need someone with deep knowledge to unlock its meanings, explain the background of revelations, and show how its rulings fit together as a complete way of life.

Studying Tafseer with a reputable and reliable source allows you to understand each verse clearly — how it connects to the life of the Prophet ﷺ, how it shaped mindsets, and how it transformed a society in need of guidance.

Your teacher

A scholar of the Quran.

Shaykh Haytham Tamim has dedicated his life to studying the Quran, drawing from major classical texts and the teachings of renowned scholars. His knowledge has not just been acquired from books, but from being in the company of respected scholars from whom the knowledge has been passed down over generations, from chest to chest, going back to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

As a community leader, he understands that sacred knowledge is meant to shape lives, not sit on shelves. His explanations break down verses to reveal their psychological, emotional, historical and legal implications.

How long is each class?

Classes run for 1.5 hours, twice weekly. Recordings are available if you cannot attend live.

Can I join online?

Yes. The class is available online and in person, so you can learn from wherever you are.

What if I miss a lesson?

Recordings are provided so you can catch up at a time that suits you.

Do I need to speak Arabic?

No. The Shaykh teaches in clear English and explains the Arabic where it matters.
