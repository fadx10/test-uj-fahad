Volunteer with us

Use your skills for the Deen.

Do you have skills you’d like to use in the service of Allah and His Book? Are you reliable and have some time to dedicate? If you would like to earn the reward and blessings of serving the Deen, and become part of Utrujj Foundation volunteers, please get in touch.

Apply to volunteer Meet the team

Why volunteer

Every effort matters.

At Utrujj, we deeply value the dedication and contributions of our volunteers, without whom our work would not be possible.

Serve the Deen

Use your time and skills in the service of Allah and His Book, and share in the reward of knowledge shared.

Join a community

Work alongside a warm, dedicated team of volunteers who care about authentic knowledge and people.

Use your gifts

From transcription and design to events and IT, there are many ways to contribute what you already do well.

Get involved

Tell us a little about you.

We would love to hear what time you have, what skills you can offer, and what you are currently doing. There is no commitment at this stage — just a conversation.
